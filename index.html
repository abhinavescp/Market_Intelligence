Forex effect: is the growth real?

A company that reports in euros, yen or Taiwan dollars can look as if it is growing faster or slower in US dollars simply because its currency got stronger or weaker. Switching this setting separates real business growth from growth flattered or dragged by the exchange rate. If a company grows much faster in dollars than in its own currency, part of that growth is currency.

Reported
Original reporting currency. No conversion; the cleanest view of underlying growth.
$ Report date
Report date exchange rate to USD: the close on the day each company announced the results.
$ Qtr avg
Quarter average exchange rate to USD: the average over the quarter, the usual way income-statement items are translated.
$ Fixed
Fixed date exchange rate to USD: one rate for every period (average of 1-7 Jan 2026), so only the business moves the numbers.
